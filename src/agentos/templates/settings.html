Approvals

Every write asks the requester to approve before it runs. Here you can turn approval off for a low-risk action — a comment on an item the requester filed, or a suggested edit to a section of a Doc, which the Doc's owner still accepts or rejects. It then runs without a card, and the thread line says no approval was needed; a comment on anyone else's item, and every other write, still shows its card. The default is requester approval, and each change is recorded in the audit log.
